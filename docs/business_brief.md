
# TrustLens – Business Brief

## Project Title
A Governed AI Analytics Assistant for E-commerce Delivery and Customer Satisfaction.

## Business Problem
An e-commerce marketplace faces delivery delays, inconsistent seller performance and customer dissatisfaction.

Business teams need a reliable way to analyze order performance, identify delivery issues and understand their impact on customer reviews.

## Project Objective
Build an analytics system that combines PostgreSQL, Python, Power BI and a Gemini-powered AI assistant to support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Stakeholders
- Head of Operations
- Seller Success Manager
- Category Manager
- Business Leadership

## Key Business Questions
1. What is the monthly late-delivery rate?
2. Which states have the highest delivery delays?
3. How do late deliveries affect customer review scores?
4. Which sellers and product categories contribute most to late deliveries?
5. How are monthly revenue, orders and average order value changing?

## Technology Stack
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Programming: Python
- Data Analysis: Pandas
- Visualization: Power BI
- AI: Gemini API
- Frontend: Streamlit
- SQL Validation: SQLGlot

## Expected Outcome
Provide reliable business insights through dashboards and a conversational AI assistant that generates and executes validated, read-only SQL queries.
